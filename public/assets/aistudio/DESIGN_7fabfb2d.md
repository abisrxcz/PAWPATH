## Brand & Style

This design system establishes an empathetic, reliable, and approachable digital environment engineered specifically for community-driven rescue, pet nutrition, and veterinary navigation across Metro Manila. 

### Visual Direction
The design ethos blends **Minimalist Warmth** with **Modern Tactile Utility**:
- **Generous Breathing Room:** High whitespace to avoid overwhelming users navigating emotionally high-stakes situations like urgent rescues or medical decisions.
- **Card-Centric Structure:** Clear physical metaphors via elevated, crisp white containers that separate critical information (adoption candidate statuses, clinic locators, diet routines) clearly over humid, dense urban lifestyles.
- **Approachable Modernity:** Clean geometric curves paired with human-centric typography that balances clinical health credibility with heartfelt animal welfare advocacy.
- **High-Affordance Touch Architecture:** Designed mobile-first for rescuers, adopters, and clinic staff navigating on handheld devices under direct Philippine sunlight and moving transit.

## Colors

The palette grounds itself in organic Philippine flora and compassionate community care, avoiding clinical sterility while retaining critical triage contrast.

### Palette Architecture
- **Primary (`#2D5A3C` - Deep Forest Green):** Anchors main actions, selected tab states, verified clinic stamps, and primary CTA buttons. Signifies vitality, flourishing health, and shelter security.
- **Secondary (`#D97736` - Warm Manila Amber):** Reserved for alert states, stray animal field reports, pending reviews, and active rescue dispatches. Provides high visibility without inducing panic.
- **Tertiary (`#5B6C8F` - Gentle Periwinkle / Slate):** Supports secondary workflows, nutrition guides, medical history logs, and non-critical care tracking.
- **Neutral Dark (`#1A1A1A` - Charcoal Bark):** Delivers WCAG AAA contrast against cream and white grounds, rendering long-form guides and vet notes effortlessly legible.
- **Canvas (`#F7F8F4` - Warm Off-White Cream):** The foundational substrate that removes harsh desktop glare and softens overall screen emissions.
- **Surface Surface (`#FFFFFF` - Pure Lily White):** Crisp floating panels, bottom sheets, and interactive cards that produce natural contrast against the cream canvas.

### Contextual Status Colors
- **Healthy / Adoptable:** Mint Green (`#34A853` with background `#E6F4EA`, text `#137333`)
- **Needs Specialized Care:** Soft Blue/Periwinkle (`#4A6FA5` with background `#EAF0F9`, text `#2A4365`)
- **Urgent Rescue / Critical:** Soft Coral Crimson (`#D9383A` with background `#FCE8E6`, text `#A50E0E`)
- **Community Stray Report:** Warm Amber (`#E37400` with background `#FEF3E6`, text `#B06000`)

## Typography

The pairing combines the open, approachable friendliness of **Plus Jakarta Sans** for titles with the systematic legibility of **Inter** for data grids, body copy, and status designations.

- **Editorial Hierarchy:** Headlines use tighter tracking and heavier weights to convey optimism and clear structure.
- **Data Densities:** Pet medical histories, feeding ratios, and location markers rely on Inter's tall x-height to maintain extreme clarity even when rendered at compact scale on low-cost mobile displays.
- **Badges & Micro-Copy:** Use `label-sm` and `label-md` with intentional uppercase transformation and letter spacing (+0.02em to +0.04em) to ensure instant glanceability in emergency rescue feeds.

## Layout & Spacing

A mobile-first fluid grid structure that seamlessly scales to desktop while preserving vertical touch discipline on hand-held devices.

### Grid & Breakpoints
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column fluid system. Gutter is `1rem` (16px), margin is `1rem` (16px). All key action zones conform to a minimum 48px touch target height.
- **Tablet (768px - 1024px):** 8-column layout. Gutter is `1rem` (16px), outer margins expand to `2rem` (32px).
- **Desktop (> 1024px):** 12-column layout. Max container width capped at `1200px` centered. Gutter expands to `1.5rem` (24px), margins to `3rem` (48px).

### Spacing Dynamics
- Use `space-xs` (4px) and `space-sm` (8px) for compact relationships: badge icon-to-label gaps, card metadata pairings, and tag groupings.
- Use `space-md` (16px) for interior card padding, row gaps, and form input stacks.
- Use `space-lg` (24px) to separate distinct card blocks or module sections.
- Use `space-xl` (40px) for prominent section divisions on scrollable feeds.

## Elevation & Depth

This system intentionally departs from heavy drop shadows to preserve battery life, eliminate visual mud on warm backgrounds, and retain a light, tropical sensibility.

### Depth Mechanics
- **Canvas Base:** `#F7F8F4` acts as level 0.
- **Card Surface (Level 1):** Solid `#FFFFFF` offset with a gentle, tinted ambient blur: `box-shadow: 0 2px 8px -2px rgba(45, 90, 60, 0.05), 0 1px 3px 0 rgba(26, 26, 26, 0.04);`.
- **Raised Interactive Cards & Floating Navigation (Level 2):** Elevated panels, bottom navigation bars, and pinned actions use `box-shadow: 0 10px 24px -4px rgba(26, 26, 26, 0.06), 0 4px 8px -2px rgba(45, 90, 60, 0.04);`.
- **Modals & Urgent Overlays (Level 3):** Rescue dispatch sheets and emergency triage modals feature `box-shadow: 0 20px 32px -8px rgba(26, 26, 26, 0.12);` complemented by a warm-tinted backdrop scrim (`rgba(26, 26, 26, 0.4)` with 4px backdrop blur).
- **Subtle Surface Definition:** Border strokes are avoided unless required for secondary button states or input borders, where a soft neutral tint (`#E5E7EB`) provides gentle, non-aggressive definition.

## Shapes

With `roundedness: 2`, the UI balances casual friendliness with structured medical utility:

- **Base Radius (0.5rem / 8px):** Applied to form text inputs, small badges, inline code snippets, and thumbnail containers.
- **Medium Radius (1rem / 16px):** Applied to pet profile cards, clinics cards, callout alerts, and bottom sheet containers.
- **Large Radius (1.5rem / 24px):** Reserved for large hero cards, onboarding carousels, and search discovery modules.
- **Full Rounded (Pill / 9999px):** Status badges, chips, category filters, and primary action buttons, inviting immediate fingertip engagement.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Background `#2D5A3C`, text `#FFFFFF`, pill-shaped (`rounded-full`), height `48px` minimum. Active state darkens to `#21432C`. Subtle tactile press transition (`transform: scale(0.98)`).
- **Secondary:** Transparent background, 1.5px solid border in `#2D5A3C`, text `#2D5A3C`. Hover/Active applies `#2D5A3C` at 6% opacity.
- **Urgent / Destructive Action:** Background `#D9383A`, text `#FFFFFF`, pill-shaped. Used for urgent dispatch claims and cancellation triggers.

### Status Badges & Chips
- **Geometry:** Height 24px (compact) or 28px (default), pill-shaped, horizontal padding `10px`. 
- **Typography:** `label-sm`, uppercase, font weight `700`.
- **Color Pairings:**
  - *HEALTHY / ADOPTABLE:* Ground `#E6F4EA`, Text `#137333`, optional 6px circular dot indicator `#34A853`.
  - *NEEDS CARE:* Ground `#EAF0F9`, Text `#2A4365`, dot `#4A6FA5`.
  - *URGENT RESCUE:* Ground `#FCE8E6`, Text `#A50E0E`, dot `#D9383A`.
  - *STRAY REPORT:* Ground `#FEF3E6`, Text `#B06000`, dot `#E37400`.
- **Interactive Chips (Filter Tags):** Inactive state has `#FFFFFF` ground, `#E5E7EB` border, and `#1A1A1A` text. Selected state has `#2D5A3C` ground and `#FFFFFF` text.

### Cards
- **Pet Adoption & Medical Card:** Ground `#FFFFFF`, border-radius `1rem`, ambient level-1 shadow. Features an edge-to-edge or 12px-padded image ratio (4:3), overlayed status badge in the upper right, bold title, age/breed tag row, and bottom metadata row (Barangay/City location and shelter name).
- **Emergency Triage Card:** Border-left `4px solid #D9383A`, ground `#FFFFFF`, with quick tap-to-call button and direct pinned location coordinates.

### Inputs & Form Controls
- **Text Inputs:** Height 48px, background `#FFFFFF`, border `1.5px solid #E2E4DE`, corner radius `0.5rem`, typography `body-md`. Focus state switches border to `#2D5A3C` with a 2px green focus ring at 15% opacity.
- **Checkboxes & Radios:** 20px size with 0.25rem corner radius (checkbox) or circular (radio). Unchecked state uses `#D1D5DB` stroke; checked state fills with `#2D5A3C` displaying a crisp white checkmark or center pip.

### Bottom Navigation Bar (Mobile)
- Fixed at viewport bottom with safe area insets. Background `#FFFFFF` with top ambient edge shadow.
- Icons use active `#2D5A3C` tint with filled glyph and `label-sm` text; inactive state uses `#6B7280` outline icon.
